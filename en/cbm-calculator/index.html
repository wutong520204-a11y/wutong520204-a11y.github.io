How it works

CBM = length × width × height in metres × quantity. For centimetres divide L × W × H by 1,000,000; for inches multiply in³ by 0.000016387.

1 CBM = 35.3147 CFT (cubic feet). Measure the outer dimensions of the packed carton or pallet, including any overhang.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate CBM from centimetres?

Multiply length × width × height in cm and divide by 1,000,000. Example: 50 × 40 × 30 cm = 60,000 cm³ = 0.06 m³. Multiply by the number of cartons for the total.

How many cubic feet are in one CBM?

One cubic metre equals about 35.315 cubic feet. To convert CFT to CBM, multiply by 0.0283168.

Should I measure the carton or the pallet?

Measure what will actually be shipped. If cartons are palletised, the forwarder charges the pallet’s outer dimensions, including the pallet base and any overhang.

Why does my forwarder’s CBM differ from mine?

Warehouses re-measure and usually round each dimension up to the next centimetre, and some round the total volume up. Small differences per carton add up over large shipments.
