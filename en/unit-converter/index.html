How it works

Exact definitions: 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg, 1 in = 2.54 cm, 1 ft = 30.48 cm, 1 ft³ = 0.028316846592 m³, 1 metric ton = 1,000 kg.

A metric ton (tonne) is 1,000 kg — not the same as a US short ton (907.18 kg) or a long ton (1,016.05 kg).

Frequently asked questions

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

1 kg = 2.20462 lb. To convert pounds to kilograms, multiply by 0.45359237.

How do I convert cubic feet to CBM?

Multiply cubic feet by 0.0283168. For example, 1,000 ft³ ≈ 28.32 m³.

Is a freight “ton” always 1,000 kg?

In international freight, “ton” normally means the metric ton of 1,000 kg. US documents may use short tons (2,000 lb), so check the contract.
