Frequently asked questions

Are these calculators free?

Yes. All tools are free, need no account and run entirely in your browser.

Can I use the results for a quote?

Use them for planning and for checking quotes. The final chargeable weight and price are set by your carrier or forwarder, who may use different divisors, minimums and rounding.

Why Russian and Chinese?

Freight between China, Central Asia and the Caucasus is usually discussed in Chinese and Russian, so each tool explains the same rules in the language your partners use.
